# Between — product steering

## What this is

A record-keeping and communication tool for separated parents who share children
and do not trust each other.

Three jobs, in the order they happen in real life:

1. **Before sending** — offer a calmer version of the message, keeping every
   fact exactly as written.
2. **After sending** — read the commitments out of the message and track whether
   the other parent confirmed them.
3. **When it breaks down** — turn the record into a dated pack someone else can
   read and independently check.

## Who it is for

A separated parent, roughly 30–50, who is already screenshotting texts "in case
I need it later". Two sub-segments:

- **The documenter** (the wedge): in or near a legal process, wants the record to
  be undeniable.
- **The de-escalator**: not in court, exhausted by the tone of every exchange.

Not the target user, and not to be designed for: family lawyers, mediators,
courts, or the other parent as an unwilling participant.

## Non-negotiable product rules

These are product decisions, not preferences. Do not quietly relax them.

1. **Between never speaks for the user.** One suggestion, clearly labelled, and
   the user chooses. "Send as written" is always visible and never disabled.
2. **Drafts are never persisted.** Only what the user actually sends enters the
   record. A hostile draft that was rewritten must not be recoverable by anyone,
   including the other parent.
3. **Facts are sacred.** A suggestion that drops or invents a date, time, amount,
   quantity or child's name is discarded before the user sees it.
4. **Threats and self-harm are never softened.** Show support resources instead,
   and still let the person send their own words.
5. **No advice.** Not legal, not medical, not parenting. Not even when asked.
6. **No claims of court admissibility.** The product shows that a record is
   unaltered; it does not prove who typed a message. Say so plainly.
7. **The export is free and complete.** A record you have to pay to read is not a
   record.
8. **No coercive-control surfaces.** No location tracking, no typing indicators,
   no tone scoring of the other parent, no "responsiveness" statistics.
9. **It must be useful to one parent alone.** Value cannot depend on the ex
   joining.

## Interface model

A messaging app, not a dashboard. Three tabs: Record, Agreements, Pack. No
sidebar, no charts, no metrics, no settings maze. The tone is paper, not product:
this is opened during the worst ten minutes of someone's week.

## Deliberately out of scope

Expense splitting, a scheduling engine, video calls, GPS, document vault, SMS or
push, native apps, lawyer or mediator seats, court filing, billing.

## How we will know it works

- Rewrite acceptance rate ≥ 50% (the core signal — if people do not use the
  suggestion, the wedge is wrong).
- Agreement confirmation rate ≥ 40%.
- D7 return with at least one new entry ≥ 35%.
- At least 0.3 evidence packs exported per active family per 30 days.
